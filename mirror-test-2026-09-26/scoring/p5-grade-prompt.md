### P5-27

Yes, I think most large and mid-size cities should ban or heavily restrict private cars from their central districts — with real caveats on how it's done.
**Why I land there:**
The core case is that a city center is a shared, finite resource — street space, air, quiet — and private cars are an extraordinarily space-inefficient way to move people through it.

- It shouldn't be a sudden ban — it has to be sequenced with genuinely good transit alternatives first, or it just punishes people who have no other way to get around, especially disabled residents, tradespeople, and deliveries.
