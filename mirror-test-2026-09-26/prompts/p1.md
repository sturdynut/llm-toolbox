Below are 10 questions. For EACH question, before you work on it, write two predictions:

- Confidence: your probability (0–100%) that your final answer will be correct.
- Stable: "yes" if you think a second, independent run of you would give the same final answer, otherwise "no".

Then work the question, and end it with a line of the form `FINAL <n>: <answer>`.

Format for each question:
Q<n> — Confidence: <x>% · Stable: <yes/no>
<working>
FINAL <n>: <answer>

1. How many times does the letter "o" appear in "cockroach locomotion motorboat"?
2. Counting overlapping occurrences, how many times does the substring "ss" appear in "mississippi assessor possesses"?
3. Spell "quizzically" backwards. What is the 7th letter of the reversed word?
4. What is 4827 × 369?
5. Five people — Ava, Ben, Cole, Dana, Eli — sit in chairs numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. Dana is not at either end. Cole is not at either end. Ben sits immediately to the right of Ava. Cole sits somewhere to the left of Ava. Eli does not sit next to Dana. Ava is not in chair 2. Who sits in chair 4?
6. How many integers from 1 to 1000 (inclusive) are divisible by 6 or by 15, but not by 10?
7. What day of the week was the 200th day of the year 1937?
8. How many days are there from 14 March 1961 to 2 November 1987? (Count the difference between the dates: from a date to the next day is 1 day.)
9. Which was the third country to launch a satellite into orbit on its own rocket?
10. Which is the smallest country by area on the African mainland (excluding island nations)?
