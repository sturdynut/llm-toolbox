**MOVE 1: Amber plays 5 -> cell 5**
**STATE 1: cell=5 visited=0,5 score Amber=5, Blue=0**

The first move of the game is always clockwise, so 0 + 5 = cell 5. Cell 5 is unvisited, and it scores 5 points.

**MOVE 2: Blue plays 1 -> cell 6**
**STATE 2: cell=6 visited=0,5,6 score Amber=5, Blue=6**

The previous tile was 5. Blue's options:

| Tile | Sum with 5 | Direction | End cell | Legal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | clockwise | 6 | yes |
| 3 | 8 | clockwise | 0 | no, visited |
| 4 | 9 | clockwise | 1 | yes |
| 6 | 11 | clockwise | 3 | yes |

I chose 1. It scores 6, and it saves Blue's big tiles for later.

**MOVE 3: Amber plays 1 -> cell 7**
**STATE 3: cell=7 visited=0,5,6,7 score Amber=12, Blue=6**

The previous tile was 1. Amber's options:

| Tile | Sum with 1 | Direction | End cell | Legal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | clockwise | 7 | yes |
| 2 | 3 | clockwise | 0 | no, visited |
| 3 | 4 | clockwise | 1 | yes |

I chose 1 because cell 7 is the highest-scoring cell.

**MOVE 4: Blue plays 4 -> cell 3**
**STATE 4: cell=3 visited=0,5,6,7,3 score Amber=12, Blue=9**

The previous tile was 1. Blue's options:

| Tile | Sum with 1 | Direction | End cell | Legal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 4 | clockwise | 7 + 3 = 10, wraps to 2 | yes |
| 4 | 5 | counter-clockwise | 7 − 4 = 3 | yes |
| 6 | 7 | clockwise | 7 + 6 = 13, wraps to 5 | no, visited |

I chose 4. It uses the counter-clockwise rule, scores 3, and leaves Amber with no legal move.

**MOVE 5: Amber plays 2 -> cell 5**
**STATE 5: cell=5 visited=0,5,6,7,3 score Amber=17, Blue=9**

The previous tile was 4, and Amber holds {2, 3}:

| Tile | Sum with 4 | Direction | End cell | Legal? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 6 | clockwise | 3 + 2 = 5 | no, visited |
| 3 | 7 | clockwise | 3 + 3 = 6 | no, visited |

Neither tile is legal, so Rule 4 applies. Amber must play her smallest tile, 2. It moves clockwise and is allowed to end on visited cell 5. Amber scores 5.

**Rule I'm least sure about: Rule 4**

The move itself follows the rule as written. However, the rule leaves some side effects open:

1. **The visited list.** Cell 5 was revisited, so I did not add it to the list a second time. The list is a set of cells, not a history of moves. If you intended it as a history, STATE 5 should read `0,5,6,7,3,5`.
2. **The next turn's direction.** Rule 4 overrides the direction for the forced move. It doesn't say whether that forced tile counts as "the tile played on the immediately previous turn" for the next player's Rule 2 check. I would assume it does, since it was still played. This doesn't affect these 5 turns, but it would on turn 6.
