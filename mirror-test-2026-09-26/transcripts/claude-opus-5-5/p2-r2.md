The first move is always clockwise, so 0 + 5 = cell 5. Amber's options were cells 1, 2, 3 or 5. I chose 5 because it scores the most.

**Rule I'm least sure I applied correctly: Rule 4.**

1. **What "can be played without breaking Rule 3" means.** I checked each tile using the direction Rule 2 would give it. Here both tiles went clockwise anyway, so the question didn't matter this game. But if a tile's legal direction were counter-clockwise, it's unclear whether Rule 4 should be judged on that direction or on some other one.
2. **How to record the forced move.** Rule 4 lets the marker end on a visited cell. I treated the visited set as unchanged, but the rules don't say whether a repeat visit should be recorded again.
3. **How the forced tile affects the next turn.** Rule 2 would presumably still use the tile 2 as "the tile played on the immediately previous turn," even though Rule 4 overrode its direction. This didn't matter because the game stopped after turn 5.

Rule 2's counter-clockwise move on turn 4 was also a spot where an error could slip in. I checked it twice: 4+1=5, and 7−4=3.
