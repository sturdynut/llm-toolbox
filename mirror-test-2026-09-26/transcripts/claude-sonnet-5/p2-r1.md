# Ring — 5 Turn Playthrough

**MOVE 1: Amber plays 2 -> cell 2**
STATE 1: cell=2 visited=0,2 score Amber=2, Blue=0

*Reasoning:* First move of the game, so per Rule 2 it's clockwise regardless of tile value. From cell 0, moving 2 clockwise: 0→1→2. Cell 2 is unvisited, so this is legal. I chose tile 2 from Amber's hand arbitrarily, saving 1, 3, 5 for later flexibility.

**MOVE 2: Blue plays 4 -> cell 6**
STATE 2: cell=6 visited=0,2,6 score Amber=2, Blue=6

*Reasoning:* Previous tile played was Amber's 2. Sum = 2+4 = 6, not a multiple of 5, so clockwise. From cell 2, +4 clockwise = cell 6. Checked all of Blue's tiles for legality first: tile 1→cell3 (legal), tile 3→cell7 (legal), tile 4→cell6 (legal), tile 6→sum=8, clockwise, cell 2+6=8 mod 8=0, which is visited — illegal. Since at least one tile was legal, Rule 4 doesn't apply. I chose tile 4.

**MOVE 3: Amber plays 3 -> cell 1**
STATE 3: cell=1 visited=0,1,2,6 score Amber=3, Blue=6

*Reasoning:* Previous tile was Blue's 4. Sum = 4+3 = 7, not multiple of 5, clockwise. From cell 6, +3 clockwise: 6→7→0→1, ending at cell 1. Cell 1 unvisited, legal. (Checked alternatives: tile 1 sum=5→counter-clockwise→cell5, also legal; tile 5 sum=9→clockwise→cell3, also legal. Chose tile 3.)

**MOVE 4: Blue plays 6 -> cell 7**
STATE 4: cell=7 visited=0,1,2,6,7 score Amber=3, Blue=13

*Reasoning:* Previous tile was Amber's 3. Sum = 3+6 = 9, not multiple of 5, clockwise. From cell 1, +6 clockwise: 1→2→3→4→5→6→7, ending at cell 7. Cell 7 unvisited, legal. (Checked tile 1: sum=4, clockwise, cell1+1=2, visited — illegal. Tile 3: sum=6, clockwise, cell1+3=4, legal. Since tile 3 and tile 6 were both legal, no Rule 4 needed. Chose tile 6.)

**MOVE 5: Amber plays 5 -> cell 4**
STATE 5: cell=4 visited=0,1,2,4,6,7 score Amber=7, Blue=13

*Reasoning:* Previous tile was Blue's 6. Amber has only {1,5} left. Sum for tile 1 = 6+1=7, clockwise, from cell 7 +1 = cell 0 mod 8 — visited, illegal. Sum for tile 5 = 6+5=11, not multiple of 5, clockwise, from cell 7 +5 = 12 mod 8 = cell 4 — unvisited, legal. Since tile 5 was legal, this was a normal (not Rule-4-forced) play, even though it was Amber's only option. Played tile 5.

**Rule I'm least sure about: Rule 2.**

My biggest uncertainty is how "the tile played on the immediately previous turn" should be read — I interpreted it as a running, cross-player sequence (i.e., Blue's direction calculation uses whatever tile Amber just played, and vice versa, regardless of whose hand it came from), rather than each player only comparing against their own last tile. The puzzle's wording supports the cross-player reading ("previous turn," not "your previous turn"), and my playthrough is internally consistent with that choice, but I never got to test the trickier edge case of what happens to this sum-chain after a Rule 4 forced move (does the forced tile still count normally as "the previous tile" for the next turn's sum, even though its own direction was forced clockwise rather than computed from Rule 2?). Since that scenario didn't arise in these 5 turns, I can't be fully confident my mental model would hold up correctly if it had.
