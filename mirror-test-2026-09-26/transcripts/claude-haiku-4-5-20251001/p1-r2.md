## Q6 — Confidence: 88% · Stable: yes

Numbers divisible by 6 or 15, but not by 10:

Divisible by 6: ⌊1000/6⌋ = 166
Divisible by 15: ⌊1000/15⌋ = 66
Divisible by both (lcm(6,15) = 30): ⌊1000/30⌋ = 33

By inclusion-exclusion: 166 + 66 - 33 = 199

Now subtract those also divisible by 10:
- Divisible by 6 and 10 (lcm = 30): 33
- Divisible by 15 and 10 (lcm = 30): 33
- Divisible by all three (lcm = 30): 33

Those divisible by (6 or 15) AND 10: 33 + 33 - 33 = 33

Result: 199 - 33 = 166

FINAL 6: 166
