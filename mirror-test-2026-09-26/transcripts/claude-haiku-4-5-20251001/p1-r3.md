## Q5 — Confidence: 85% · Stable: yes

**Logic puzzle: Who sits in chair 4?**

Constraints: Dana not at ends, Cole not at ends, Ben immediately right of Ava, Cole left of Ava, Eli not next to Dana, Ava not in chair 2.

From "Ben immediately right of Ava" and "Ava not in 2": possible (Ava, Ben) pairs are (1,2), (3,4), or (4,5).

Testing (4,5): Cole must be left of Ava (position 4), and not at positions 1 or 5, so Cole ∈ {2,3}.
- If Cole = 2: Remaining spots 1,3 for Dana and Eli. Dana ≠ 1 or 5, so Dana = 3, Eli = 1. Check: Eli(1) not next to Dana(3)? ✓
- If Cole = 3: Dana and Eli at 1,2. Dana must = 2, Eli = 1. But then Eli(1) and Dana(2) are adjacent. ✗

Testing (1,2): Cole < 1 is impossible. ✗

Testing (3,4): Cole ∈ {1,2}, but Cole ≠ 1, so Cole = 2. Remaining: 1,5 for Dana/Eli. But Dana ≠ 1 or 5. ✗

Valid arrangement: Eli(1), Cole(2), Dana(3), **Ava(4)**, Ben(5)

FINAL 5: Ava

## Q6 — Confidence: 80% · Stable: yes

**Count integers 1-1000 divisible by 6 or 15, but not by 10**

Numbers divisible by 6: ⌊1000/6⌋ = 166
Numbers divisible by 15: ⌊1000/15⌋ = 66
Numbers divisible by lcm(6,15)=30: ⌊1000/30⌋ = 33

By inclusion-exclusion: 166 + 66 - 33 = 199 divisible by 6 or 15

Now exclude those also divisible by 10:
- Divisible by both 6 and 10 = divisible by lcm(6,10)=30: 33
- Divisible by both 15 and 10 = divisible by lcm(15,10)=30: 33
- These are the same set (both are lcm=30)

Answer: 199 - 33 = 166

FINAL 6: 166

## Q7 — Confidence: 80% · Stable: yes

**What day was the 200th day of 1937?**

The 200th day: Jan(31) + Feb(28) + Mar(31) + Apr(30) + May(31) + Jun(30) = 181 days → July 19

Using Zeller's congruence for July 19, 1937:
- h = (19 + ⌊13(8)/5⌋ + 37 + ⌊37/4⌋ + ⌊19/4⌋ - 2·19) mod 7
- h = (19 + 20 + 37 + 9 + 4 - 38) mod 7
- h = 51 mod 7 = 2

h=2 corresponds to Monday

FINAL 7: Monday
